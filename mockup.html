Study notes · Alma 32 — Friday, July 17

Faith is a seed — it only grows if I plant it and nourish it every day. Alma doesn't promise instant fruit; he promises that if I don't cast it out, it swells and sprouts. Tie it to the build: patience with Companion, patience with people. Where am I casting out the seed by neglect? The morning study is the nourishing — don't skip it.
